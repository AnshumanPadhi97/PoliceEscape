# Police Escape

Drive your getaway car through an endless city. The police chase you, oil slicks lie in your way, and the longer you survive the more trouble comes. Collect cash, grab power-ups, spend it in the shop, and beat your best score.

## How to play

Stay alive and collect cash. Your **score = seconds survived + cash collected**. Your best score is saved.

You are busted if you hit a **building**, a **police car**, a **bullet**,  a **civilian car**. **Armor** absorbs one hit (not buildings), and **nitro** makes you briefly untouchable (but not against buildings).

The trick is to make cops crash themselves: lead them into buildings, parked cars, traffic and each other. Killing gives no points, but it clears the way.

### Controls

| Action | Phone / touch | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Steer | Hold and drag: the car turns toward your finger | `←` `→` or `A` `D` |
| Nitro | NITRO button (bottom right) | `Space` or `Shift` |
| Pause / resume | Pause button (top right) | `P` or `Esc` |
| Start / retry | PLAY and Retry buttons | `Enter` or `Space` on a menu |

The game pauses by itself when you leave the app or switch windows.

## The city

| Thing | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Buildings** | Solid. Touching one ends your run. Most blocks are buildings. |
| **Parks and parking lots** | Open blocks you can cut through. Trees and parked cars are solid, but armor or nitro saves you. |
| **Oil slicks** | Your car loses grip and keeps sliding the way it was going. Steer before you get there. |
| **Cones and debris** | Slow you down by about 45% (nitro ignores it). |
| **Civilian traffic** | Sedans, vans, taxis, buses, trucks, ambulances and scooters drive along the lanes. Long ones like buses are solid along their whole length. Hitting any of them busts you, but cops that hit them crash. |
| **Pedestrians** | People walk the sidewalks, alone or in groups, and some stand around chatting. They run away when you get close. They are just for show and are harmless. |
| **Cash trails** | Curved lines of cash. Collecting them is your score and your money. |
| **Day and night** | A full day lasts 2 minutes and each run starts at a random time of day. Dusk turns the city orange, then night falls: the city goes dark except for your headlights, the street lamps at the corners and the glow of police lights. |
| **Rain and fog** | Weather rolls in now and then. Fog hides everything outside a circle around your car. |

## The police

Cops spawn just off-screen and chase you. Arrows at the screen edge point to ones you cannot see. Most cops steer around buildings, but not all of them.

There are three police cars, three police bikes and a helicopter.

| Unit | Appears at | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Patrol car** | start | Fast homing chaser. Gives up after about 15 seconds. |
| **SWAT van** | 22 s | Circles you at range and fires bullets every 2.2 seconds. Bullets stop at buildings. |
| **Interceptor** | 40 s | Stops, shows a flashing red line, then charges along it at high speed. |
| **Chopper** (bike) | 14 s | Steady, heavier bike that chases you. |
| **Motorcycle** | 30 s | Very fast with sharp turns. |
| **Gunbike** | 35 s | Circles you on a bike and fires bullets every 2.6 seconds. |
| **Helicopter** | 45 s | Flies over everything and calls airstrikes: a red circle appears, then it blasts. Only the EMP can bring it down. |

### Bosses

The first boss arrives at about 100 seconds, then roughly every 110 seconds after you defeat one. They alternate and get more health each time. A defeated boss drops a burst of cash. Bosses smash through buildings.

- **Armored tank**: keeps its distance, fires a fan of three shells and two homing rockets.
- **Riot dozer**: chases you, slams the ground in a flashing circle.

Bosses take damage from the EMP (4) and cops crashing into them (1).

## Difficulty

Difficulty comes in **surges**. Nothing ever gets faster: only the number of cops changes. Every 45 seconds the trouble rises to a peak, then drops back to a calm, and each peak is bigger than the last. Use the calm to collect cash and power-ups.

## Abilities and power-ups

- **Nitro**: a short burst of speed (about 0.35 s) that makes you untouchable. It recharges in 3 seconds.
- **Armor** 🛡: absorbs one hit. **Turbo** ⚡: 40% faster for 8 s. **Magnet** 🧲: pulls cash from three times as far. **Slow-mo** ⏱: cops and bullets at half speed. **EMP** 💥: wrecks every cop in range.
